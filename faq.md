**How many people and sessions can we have?**
As many as you like – there is no limit on participants or sessions. A session holds up to 300 work items.

**Do participants need special permissions?**
No. Everyone sees the work items Jira lets them see. Saving needs the permission to edit the work items.

**Which estimate fields work?**
"Story point estimate" of team-managed spaces, "Story Points" of company-managed spaces, any other number field, the original time estimate, single-line text fields and the priority. In company-managed spaces the field must be on the edit screen of the work type.

**Someone changed an estimate in Jira during our session. Will we overwrite it?**
Not silently. Save to Jira lists such work items and keeps Jira's value unless you tick them.

**Can votes be changed after the reveal?**
Yes. A moderator can also start another round for a card with "Vote again".

**Does the app store our work items?**
No. It stores the ids of the work items, the lanes, the votes and the account ids of participants. Summaries and other content are read in your browser and never stored.

**How long are sessions kept?**
Until a moderator deletes them, or 180 days after the last change.

**Is there a chat?**
No – the app deliberately stores nothing people write. Use your meeting or comments on the work items.

**We use a Connect-based estimation app. Can we move?**
Yes. Sessions are short-lived, so there is nothing to migrate: start a new session from the same backlog, sprint or JQL.

**Is our data sent anywhere?**
No. The app runs on Atlassian Forge and has no access to anything outside Atlassian.
