**Team Estimation** lets a team size its Jira work items together – on one shared board, or with planning poker – and saves the agreed estimates to Jira.

Estimating one work item at a time is slow. Put the whole backlog on a board instead:

- **Sizing board** – every card on one board, everyone drags the cards to their size at the same time. Changes of the others appear live. Dozens of work items are sized in minutes.
- **Planning poker, live or async** – everyone places the cards in private, a moderator reveals the votes. Cards everyone agrees on go straight to their size; the rest show who voted what, so you only discuss the differences. Votes can be collected in a meeting or over a few days.
- **Take turns** and **relative sizing** – the classic "magic estimation" game: one card per person, lanes from small to large, values at the end.
- **Any estimate field** – story points in team-managed and company-managed spaces, any number field, the original time estimate, a text field for T-shirt sizes, or the priority.
- **Safe saving** – before writing, the app compares with Jira: estimates changed in Jira during the session are shown and kept unless you decide otherwise.
- **Starts where you work** – from the backlog, a sprint or a work item search, or from Apps › Estimation Sessions.

## Safe by design
- Work items are read and written **with each person's own Jira permissions**.
- The app stores **ids only** – no summaries or other content of work items. No data leaves Atlassian. **Runs on Atlassian.**
